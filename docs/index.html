SUPPORT

Common questions.

Will my earbuds work with MiBudsClient?

Redmi Buds 6 Play is the hardware reference for the project. On other Xiaomi and Redmi models, available controls and connection support depend on the model and firmware. ANC and transparency are model-specific and still need hardware verification across devices.

How do I connect for the first time?

Pair and connect your earbuds in your computer’s Bluetooth settings, then launch MiBudsClient. It looks for connected, recognized devices and shows the controls available for your model.

Can I change my touch controls?

Open Settings and choose Gestures to view the left and right assignments. The available actions and gestures depend on your earbuds.

Is MiBudsClient free?

Yes. MiBudsClient is an independent, open-source community project licensed under GPL-3.0. You can explore the source code and release history on GitHub.
